# ADR-004: Dialogflow CX as Conversational Platform

## Status

Accepted

---

## Context

Project Phoenix requires a conversational platform capable of managing complex dialogue flows and integrating with backend services.

---

## Decision

Dialogflow CX will manage conversational interactions.

---

## Alternatives Considered

- Dialogflow ES
- Microsoft Bot Framework
- Rasa

---

## Reasons

- Flow-based conversation modeling
- Enterprise-scale conversation management
- Native webhook support
- Google Cloud integration

---

## Consequences

Advantages

- Better support for complex conversational workflows
- Easier maintenance of conversation flows

Trade-offs

- Slightly steeper learning curve than simpler chatbot platforms.
