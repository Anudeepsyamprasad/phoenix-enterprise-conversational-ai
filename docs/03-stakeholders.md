# Stakeholders

## Overview

Project Phoenix involves multiple stakeholders, each interacting with the platform in different ways. Understanding their goals helps define system requirements and design decisions.

---

## Primary Stakeholders

### 1. Customer

The customer interacts with the chatbot to obtain information or complete simple tasks.

#### Goals

- Receive quick and accurate responses
- Avoid long waiting times
- Complete tasks without human assistance

#### Needs

- Easy-to-use interface
- Reliable responses
- Secure handling of personal information

---

### 2. Administrator

Administrators configure and monitor the platform.

#### Responsibilities

- Review conversations
- Manage chatbot configuration
- Monitor usage
- View analytics

---

### 3. Developer

Developers maintain the application.

#### Responsibilities

- Build new features
- Maintain APIs
- Fix bugs
- Deploy updates
- Monitor system health

---

## Secondary Stakeholders

### Business Owner

Defines business objectives and approves new features.

### Support Team

Uses conversation history to resolve issues that cannot be handled automatically.

### Cloud Provider

Hosts the deployed application and provides infrastructure services.

---

## Stakeholder Summary

| Stakeholder | Primary Goal |
|-------------|--------------|
| Customer | Obtain information quickly |
| Administrator | Manage the platform |
| Developer | Build and maintain the application |
| Business Owner | Improve operational efficiency |
| Support Team | Handle escalated conversations |
